# Customer-Analysis-Dashboard

The Customer Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance and customer behavior. It aggregates detailed order information to present key insights such as total revenue generated and its distribution across different US states. The dashboard highlights month-wise revenue trends, revealing fluctuations and potential seasonality, and analyzes sales across different age groups. It also breaks down the regional revenue share, identifying the strongest contributing areas, and provides a gender-wise analysis of sales across various product categories. Furthermore, the dashboard explores the correlation between the quantity of items ordered and the discount percentage applied.

Therefore, by integrating the high-level visualizations of the dashboard with the granular details of the order data, the project aims to provide actionable insights into sales drivers, customer preferences, and areas for potential optimization. This holistic view empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions regarding marketing strategies, inventory management, and customer relationship management, ultimately contributing to enhanced business performance and revenue growth.
